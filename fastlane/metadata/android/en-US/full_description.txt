P2PMine is the official mobile dashboard for the p2pmine.com Bitcoin mining pool.

• Live hashrate and unpaid balance
• Worker list with stratum setup
• Earnings and payout history
• Request a payout to your BTC address
• Optional biometric lock and two-factor authentication
• Home-screen widget for hashrate, balance, and worker count

Earnings shown in the app are after the published pool fee. The app talks only to p2pmine.com over HTTPS.
